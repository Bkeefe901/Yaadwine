Why Every Bottle
Has a Journey

We never wanted YaadWine to be another wine review page.

Here's the truth: we got tired of the wine world acting like everything just... appeared. Like a grape simply belongs somewhere, the way a fact belongs in an encyclopedia. Malbec is Argentine. Chenin Blanc is from the Loire. Full stop. End of sentence. Nothing more to ask.

But nothing in wine just appeared.

Someone had to carry a vine across an ocean.

Someone had to learn a new climate the hard way, one bad harvest at a time.

Someone built the irrigation that makes the whole wine system possible, and their name isn't on the label.

Someone bet everything on a grape that history had already given up on.

I think about this because we all came from somewhere and something, then evolve into this.

I'm Jamaican—heritage and birth—living in New York, and I got into wine here, immersed in a world that mostly doesn't look like me or think about people who look like me. And that's changing.

So when we started digging into wine, we kept noticing the same pattern everywhere we looked.

Grapes have migration stories.

Regions have migration stories.

And almost always, if you dig one layer past the tasting notes, there's a person—or a whole people—who did the actual work and never got the credit. Sometimes they chose to move. Sometimes they didn't get a choice at all. Either way, most wine writing skips straight past them to get to the part about tannins and the many likes on social media.

We won't skip that part.

That's YaadWine.

Not a wine blog.

Not tasting notes with jokes attached... well, maybe you'll see some of that.

This is where we tell you the whole story of a bottle—the fun parts and the parts that make you sit for a second, think, and drink on it—because we think you deserve the whole truth, not just the version that gets the most likes.

Every bottle has a journey.

We just happen to think you should know who also made the journey with the bottle.

— Welcome to YaadWine.
