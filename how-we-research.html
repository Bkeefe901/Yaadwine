How We Research

What's Behind
Every Story

YaadWine explores wine through history, culture, science, and personal storytelling. Every piece we publish is grounded in real research — historical records, scientific sources, and cultural documentation — checked before it goes on the page, not after.

Where a story includes technical or historical claims, we do the work to verify them. Where the historical record is genuinely uncertain or disputed among researchers, we say so, directly in the piece, rather than presenting one version as settled fact.

The reader gets the journey. We keep the map.

Most of our pieces include a Sources & Further Reading section at the end — not a full academic bibliography, but a real starting point for anyone who wants to explore the history behind the story further.

Some things worth knowing as you read:

  • Facts are checked against real sources before publication.
  • Interpretation and cultural meaning are ours — clearly framed as perspective, not fact.
  • Oral history and cultural tradition are treated with respect, even where they can't be fully verified in writing.
  • Some traditions have multiple, sometimes conflicting, cultural or historical perspectives — we try to hold space for that rather than flatten it.
  • If we get something wrong, we fix it.
